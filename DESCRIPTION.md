# YippYapp Helper

**The gear maths the game hides.** Midnight Season 2.

Which crest should you spend, and on what? Is that +12 key actually improving
anything? WoW doesn't tell you. This does.

---

### Stop wasting crests

Every slot gets a verdict — **Upgrade Now**, **Hold**, **Save for a Drop** —
weighing crest scarcity against what you're about to replace anyway. It warns
you *before* the wasteful spend, not after: Hero 5→6 and Myth 1→2 cover the
same item levels at the same price, and only one of those crests goes dead in
three weeks.

Opens itself beside the upgrade vendor. Click a slot to upgrade it.

### Know where the ceiling is

+10, +11 and +12 all drop the same item level and all fill the same vault.
Above +10 you are farming rating, not gear. The game never says this.

### Meet Mr. Yeeper

He reads your gear, crests, vault, bags and gold, works out the single most
damning thing that is true, and says it. Not a quote generator — every insult
is an argument from your own numbers.

> 700k gold and four slots with no enchant on them. What are you saving it for?

### Best in Slot

Your spec's list on a paper doll, so you can see the gaps rather than read
them. Items in your bags count as collected.

### Also included

**Mythic+** — dungeon overview, party rating grid, group and guild keystones
synced automatically, vault tracker, rating goals · **Loot Browser** — every
dungeon, raid and world-boss drop by slot, for any class · **Trinkets** —
SimulationCraft rankings, plus which specs want the one you just looted ·
**Raid Tools** — group splits, swap suggestions, release blocker, addon
scanner · **Ready Check** — food, flask, stats and durability for every raider
in one grid · **Teleports** — every Hero's Path port, MoP to Midnight ·
**Consumables** — enchants, gems, flasks and food per spec · **Interrupt
tracker**, **utility advisor** and **completion popup** (beta)

Everything lives in one window, movable through Blizzard's own Edit Mode.

### Commands

| | |
|---|---|
| `/yh` | Open it |
| `/yh help` | Every command |
| `/yh shell <page>` | Straight to a page |
| `/yh guide` | Boss guide for the current raid |
| `/yh train [boss]` | Practise a boss's mechanics |
| `/yh settings` | Options |
